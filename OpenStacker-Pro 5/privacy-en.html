Privacy information

This page describes the technical behavior of the OpenStacker Pro demo distribution. Adapt and review it before collecting real user data.

Last updated: 9 October 2026

1. Local mode

Without Supabase configuration, favorites, theme, language, demo ratings, demo comments and submissions are stored in the browser's local storage. They are not sent to an OpenStacker database and are not synced across devices. Clearing site data in your browser may remove them.

2. Optional backend

If the site operator installs and configures Supabase, authentication, profile information, ratings, comments and submissions are sent to the configured Supabase project. The project owner controls retention, access, region and deletion. The included Row Level Security policies are a starting point and must be reviewed before production.

3. External services and network requests

The site may load fonts from Google Fonts, favicons from Google's favicon endpoint and website screenshots through image.thum.io. These services receive the requests required to provide those resources and may process technical data such as IP address, user-agent and requested URL according to their own policies. Linked vendors operate under their own terms and privacy notices.

4. No built-in advertising or profiling

The project does not include an advertising pixel, analytics tracker or profiling system by default. Anyone modifying or distributing the code must update this notice when adding analytics, marketing or other services.

5. Data and rights

This page is not a substitute for a privacy notice required by applicable law. Before commercial publication, the data controller should identify themselves, provide valid contact details, state legal bases, retention periods, processors, international transfers and procedures for exercising data-subject rights. Do not enter sensitive information or unnecessary third-party personal data in this demo.

6. Contact and updates

A public release should provide an up-to-date contact for the data controller. Adapt this document to the features and configuration actually in use.
